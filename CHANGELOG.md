# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Cross Media Bar home screen in the style of RetroArch's XMB: Favorites, Recent, Apps, Live, Calendar, Weather,
  Multiview, Inputs and Settings categories, 15 colour themes, ribbon / simple ribbon / snow / bokeh backgrounds,
  frosted top edge, submenus, quick menus, on-screen notices and optional menu sounds.
- Live TV: IPTV (M3U) playlists, TV guide, channel changing, resume last channel.
- Multiview: one to four live windows (HDMI inputs, web pages, IPTV channels, widgets) with quick actions that fade
  when idle, full screen, and a way back to the wall.
- Weather (Open-Meteo) and calendar (public `.ics` links, including Apple Calendar).
- Ambient sleep clock that dims the panel after five idle minutes.
- Every setting lives on the TV, and can also be changed from a phone or computer through *Phone setup*: PIN
  protected, off until switched on, and it turns itself off after 15 minutes.
- Version number shown in Settings → About.
- Optional Homebridge plugin for Apple Home control of Hisense / Google TV devices.
